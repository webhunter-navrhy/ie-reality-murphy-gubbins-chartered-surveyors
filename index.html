About the property

Description property

Castleconnell is a picturesque riverside village on the River Shannon, situated on the Limerick–Clare border and just 15km from Limerick City. Steeped in history, the village takes its name from the Castle of Connell, the ruins of which stand overlooking the Shannon. The village offers a wonderful range of local amenities, including cafés, pubs, restaurants, a national school, church, post office, Garda station, neighbourhood centre and train station. Its beautiful riverside setting provides an abundance of recreational opportunities, from fishing and rowing to scenic walks along the Shannon and through the nearby Fairy Woods.

Castleconnell is renowned for its natural beauty and wildlife, including the swans that migrate from Iceland each winter. The village is also home to the Castle Oaks Hotel, Castleconnell Rowing Club and is adjacent to the highly successful Ahane Hurling Club, giving the community a vibrant and active atmosphere.

With excellent transport links to Limerick City, Nenagh and Dublin, Castleconnell offers the perfect balance of village living, natural surroundings and easy access to major employment, education and transport hubs.

This family home is superbly positioned adjacent to Castleconnell School and Train Station. It enjoys a highly convenient setting on the approach to the village. Offering generous accommodation, lovely mature gardens and excellent potential for modernisation, the property also benefits from a spacious driveway extending around to the rear and a detached shed.

Castleconnell village and its range of amenities are all within a short stroll.

Accommondation comprises entrance hallway, 2 living room, kitchen, utility/wc, back porch, 3 bedrooms and shower room.
